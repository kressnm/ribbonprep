**Last updated: November 24, 2026** (added the Ribbon Prep Pro purchase)

Ribbon Prep ("the app") is an independent study aid for people preparing for the Oncology Certified Nurse (OCN) exam. It is made by the Ribbon Prep developer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Oncology Nursing Certification Corporation (ONCC).

**The short version:** the app has no accounts and no cross-app tracking. What you enter (your name, answers, notes) stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in a backup in your own iCloud account that only you can see. It is never sent to the developer. The app sends anonymous usage counts, such as which screens are opened, that can't be tied to you; you can turn this off in Settings. The developer does not sell any information.

## What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your phone:

- Your first name, exam date, and daily question goal, if you enter them
- Which practice questions you answered, whether you got them right, and when missed questions are due for review
- How many questions you answered each day (for the daily goal)
- Your flashcard progress
- Questions and cards you starred, and notes you write
- Your practice exam scores, and a practice exam in progress so you can resume it
- Your settings, such as appearance, haptics, and reminder time

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it from your phone (an iCloud backup, if you have one, stays in your iCloud; see below). The reset option in Settings erases your answers and scores (notes and stars are kept).

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
- It does not use advertising or tracking, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not send your name, answers, notes, or anything you type.
- It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or health information.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Anonymous usage data

To learn which parts of the app are useful, the app sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service (telemetrydeck.com). It is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings, under Preferences, Share anonymous usage.

- **What is sent:** which screens are opened; a few actions, such as starting, pausing, or finishing a practice exam (with its score, length, and time used), answering the Question of the Day, or saving a backup; and the app version, iOS version, device model, and language.
- **What is never sent:** your name, exam date, answers to questions, notes, search text, or anything you type.
- **How it stays anonymous:** the app creates a random ID on your phone (not your device's identifier or your Apple account) and scrambles it with a one-way hash before sending, so events can't be linked to you or your device. TelemetryDeck hashes it again on their side. The data is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared.

On the App Store this is listed as "Data Not Linked to You: Usage Data."

## Automatic iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, the app saves a backup of the information listed above (except a practice exam in progress) to your own iCloud account once a day and when you leave the app. It keeps the newest seven daily backups. This is so you can get your progress back after reinstalling the app or on a new phone; the app offers to restore it the first time it opens.

- The backup is stored in your iCloud account, under Apple's iCloud terms. The developer cannot see, open, or receive it.
- Turn it off in the app (Settings, iCloud backup) or in your phone's Settings (your name, iCloud). To delete it, go to your phone's Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), Ribbon Prep.

## Notifications and the Lock Screen timer

If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. If notifications are allowed, a practice exam can also send "time left" alerts. Both are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet to create them.

During a practice exam, a countdown can appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It is drawn on your phone by iOS and is not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings, under Ribbon Prep, Live Activities.

## Things that leave your phone only when you choose

- **Backups:** Settings, then Save a backup, creates a file with your progress, notes, stars, scores, and settings. It is saved wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Files on your phone. If you save it to iCloud Drive, Apple's terms for iCloud apply. The developer never receives it.
- **Reporting a problem or suggesting an idea:** the app prepares an email (or a share sheet). It is sent only if you choose to send it, from your own email app. It contains the question or card you are reporting and anything you write. It is used only to fix errors and improve the app.
- **Links:** source and reference links open official websites (for example ONCC, NCI, and FDA) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
- **Ratings and sharing:** rating the app and sharing it happen through Apple's standard screens.

## Ribbon Prep Pro purchase

Ribbon Prep Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never receives your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The app asks Apple only whether this Apple Account owns Pro, so it can unlock it (and when you tap Restore Purchases). Anonymous usage data includes whether Pro is unlocked, so the developer can count how many people use each version.

## Apple

Apple may collect limited information about app downloads and, if you turned on sharing with app developers on your phone, crash and usage reports. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings. The developer does not receive your personal information from it.

## Children

The app is made for adult nursing students and nurses and is not directed to children under 13.

## Not medical advice

The questions, flashcards, and quick sheets are for exam study only. They are not medical advice and must not be used to make decisions about patient care. Always follow your facility's policies and current official guidelines.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated, and any meaningful change will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
