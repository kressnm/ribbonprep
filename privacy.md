**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

Ribbon Prep ("the app") is an independent study aid for people preparing for the Oncology Certified Nurse (OCN) exam. It is made by the Ribbon Prep developer. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Oncology Nursing Certification Corporation (ONCC).

**The short version:** the app has no accounts, no servers, and no tracking. What you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to send or save it somewhere. The Ribbon Prep developer does not collect, see, or sell your information.

## What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

- Your first name, exam date, and daily question goal, if you enter them
- Which practice questions you answered, whether you got them right, and when missed questions are due for review
- How many questions you answered each day (for the daily goal)
- Your flashcard progress
- Questions and cards you starred, and notes you write
- Your practice exam scores, and a practice exam in progress so you can resume it
- Your settings, such as appearance, haptics, and reminder time

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. The reset option in Settings erases your answers and scores (notes and stars are kept).

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
- It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or health information.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Notifications and the Lock Screen timer

If you turn on the daily study reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. If notifications are allowed, a practice exam can also send "time left" alerts. Both are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet to create them.

During a practice exam, a countdown can appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It is drawn on your phone by iOS and is not sent anywhere. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings, under Ribbon Prep, Live Activities.

## Things that leave your phone only when you choose

- **Backups:** Settings, then Save a backup, creates a file with your progress, notes, stars, scores, and settings. It is saved wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Files on your phone. If you save it to iCloud Drive, Apple's terms for iCloud apply. The developer never receives it.
- **Reporting a problem or suggesting an idea:** the app prepares an email (or a share sheet). It is sent only if you choose to send it, from your own email app. It contains the question or card you are reporting and anything you write. It is used only to fix errors and improve the app.
- **Links:** source and reference links open official websites (for example ONCC, NCI, and FDA) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
- **Purchases, ratings, and sharing:** buying the optional Pro upgrade, rating the app, and sharing it all happen through Apple's standard screens. The developer does not receive your payment details.

## Apple

Apple may collect limited information about app downloads and, if you turned on sharing with app developers on your phone, crash and usage reports. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings. The developer does not receive your personal information from it.

## Children

The app is made for adult nursing students and nurses and is not directed to children under 13.

## Not medical advice

The questions, flashcards, and quick sheets are for exam study only. They are not medical advice and must not be used to make decisions about patient care. Always follow your facility's policies and current official guidelines.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated, and any meaningful change will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
