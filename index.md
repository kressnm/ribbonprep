Ribbon Prep is an independent study aid for the Oncology Certified Nurse (OCN) exam. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ONCC.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, or ideas: **[support.ribbonprep@gmail.com](mailto:support.ribbonprep@gmail.com)**

Found a mistake in a question or flashcard? Open it in the app and tap **Report a problem**. It fills in the details for you. Please say what you think is wrong and, if you can, include a source.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my progress saved if I delete the app?**
Yes, if you use iCloud. The app backs up your progress, notes, stars, and scores to your own iCloud once a day and when you leave the app (only you can see it). Reinstall the app and it offers to restore the backup. Without iCloud, go to Settings, then Save a backup, and keep the file in Files.

**How do I move my progress to a new phone?**
If both phones use the same iCloud account, install Ribbon Prep on the new phone and tap Restore when it offers your iCloud backup (or go to Settings, then Restore from iCloud). Without iCloud: on your old phone, go to Settings, then Save a backup; on the new phone, go to Settings, then Restore from a backup, and choose that file.

**Where do the questions come from?**
They are original questions written for this app, each with a rationale and, where possible, a link to an official source such as NCI, FDA drug labels, or national guidelines. Questions marked **Draft** have not yet been reviewed by an oncology nurse.

**Is the practice exam the same as the real OCN exam?**
No. It mirrors the real exam's length (165 questions in 3 hours) and topic mix as a practice tool. The real exam reports a scaled score, so the percentage in the app is only a rough guide.

**Can I pause a practice exam?**
Yes. Tap the pause button on the exam screen, or on the countdown on your Lock Screen. The real OCN exam can't be paused, so practice without pausing when you can.

**How do I turn off reminders?**
Settings, then switch off Daily study reminder. You can also turn off notifications for Ribbon Prep in your phone's Settings.

**How do I reset my progress?**
Settings, then Reset practice progress. Your notes and stars are kept.

**Does the app cost anything or show ads?**
Ribbon Prep is free to download. The free version includes the Question of the Day, a sample of practice questions and a sample exam, every flashcard, Quick Sheets, Lab Values, References, search, notes, and stars. Ribbon Prep Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks every question, full-length timed exams with score history, custom practice sets, the review schedule, Eliminate two, Exam Readiness, and the Drug Reference. There are no ads, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites. It does send anonymous usage data, such as which screens are opened, to help improve the app. This is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings, under Preferences, Share anonymous usage. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy/) for details.

**I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone or after reinstalling?**
Pro comes back by itself on any iPhone signed into the same Apple Account. If it doesn't, go to Settings, Ribbon Prep Pro, then Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
